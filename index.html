How to get a cleaner reading

Sit quietly for about 5 minutes. Keep your back supported, feet flat, legs uncrossed, arm supported at heart level, and use the correct cuff size. Avoid talking during the measurement.

When is it urgent?

If your blood pressure is higher than 180 systolic and/or higher than 120 diastolic, repeat the measurement after a brief rest and follow your screening team's protocol.

Call 911 for emergency symptoms such as chest pain, shortness of breath, new weakness or numbness, vision changes, difficulty speaking, or other severe symptoms with a very high reading.
High blood pressure categories follow the 2025 American Heart Association/American College of Cardiology adult blood pressure guideline: Normal <120 and <80; Elevated 120–129 and <80; Stage 1 130–139 or 80–89; Stage 2 ≥140 or ≥90. For this project, a separate low screening range is defined as systolic <90 and diastolic <60.

This page is for adult community education and screening, not diagnosis or individualized treatment. Pregnancy requires pregnancy-specific blood pressure guidance.

Sources: American Heart Association: Understanding Blood Pressure Readings · 2025 High Blood Pressure Guideline
